                                        FOREWORD



     This report, prepared as part of the duties entrusted to the Director General
of Elections under Section 26 (5) of the Electoral Code, relates the conduct
of the 12 March 2023 election of Senators in a chronological order. The report
provides an exhaustive account of activities carried out by ELECAM and other
stakeholders of the electoral process.
   Part I of this report is a narration, while Part II comprises a compilation of
annexes that constitute an important and additional source for information.
     The intent of this report is to provide readers with ample objective facts
they can use to appraise efforts made by stakeholders to ensure the election
of the third batch of 70 senators representing the regional and local authorities
that make up the Senate.
    I hope all readers of this report find it satisfactory.




                                                Dr ESSOUSSE Erik,
                                                DIRECTOR GENERAL OF ELECTIONS,
