                                                                                                         FOREWORD




                                                         FOREWORD

                               This report, drawn up as part of the duties entrusted
                          to the Director General of Elections under Section 26 (5)
                          of the Electoral Code, describes the organisation and
                          conduct of the twin legislative and municipal elections of
                          9 February 2020 and the legislative rerun of 22 March 2020
                          in 10 (ten) constituencies of the North-West and 01 (one)
                          of the South-West.
                              The report offers a blow-by-blow account of actions
                          accomplished by the different stakeholders in regard to
                          organising and managing operations related to the said
                          elections, pins down essential technical hitches and
                          formulates recommendations on ways and means of
                          improving thereupon in future elections.
                              In due course and for the record, the report relates
                          other milestones marking progress towards democracy in
                          Cameroon and offers enough facts valuable to any in-
                          depth assessment of democracy as well as the
                          participation of every single stakeholder.
                              The intent of this document, per se, is to enable
                          readers form their own judgement on efforts made
                          towards improving our electoral process!


                                                                          DIRECTOR GENERAL OF ELECTIONS
